About

We teach the boring parts on purpose.

Most trading content sells excitement. We sell process — the unglamorous discipline of risk sizing and rule-following that separates people who last from people who blow up in month three.

How the academy started

  1. 2019

    A trading floor, not a classroom

    Founded by four former prop desk traders who kept getting asked the same beginner questions and got tired of answering them one-on-one.

  2. 2021

    The four-track structure

    Rebuilt the whole curriculum around one idea: risk management has to come before strategy, not after it.

  3. 2026

    3,000+ graduates

    Still taught by practitioners who trade their own accounts, not full-time instructors who stopped.
