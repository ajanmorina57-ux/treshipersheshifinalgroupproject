About

We teach the boring parts on purpose.

Most trading content sells excitement. We teach process, risk management, and the discipline needed to follow a trading plan.

How the Academy Started

  1. 2019 - A Trading Floor, Not a Classroom

    Founded by four former prop desk traders who kept getting asked the same beginner questions.

  2. 2021 - The Four-Track Structure

    The curriculum was rebuilt around one idea: risk management should come before strategy.

  3. 2026 - 3,000+ Graduates

    The courses are still taught by practitioners who trade their own accounts.
